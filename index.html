Food and handwork from the Gurez valley

We're a small family business in Gurez, Kashmir. We sell what our valley grows and makes: raw honey, our own rajma, walnuts, kahwa and warm woollens.

Shop the harvest See where it comes from

Gurez sits at 8,000 feet, on the far side of the Razdan Pass.

For about five months of the year, snow closes the only road out. The rest of the year we farm, gather and store everything we'll need for winter.

What grows here is different from what you get in the plains. Our rajma is small and yellow, the walnuts come from old trees around the village, and the honey changes with whatever is flowering on the slopes.
